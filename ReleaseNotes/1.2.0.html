Ataraxia 1.2.0

  • Software updates: Ataraxia can now check for and install updates itself (Ataraxia → Check for Updates…, or Settings → General → Software Updates). Every update and the update feed are signed and verified before anything is installed.
  • Card buttons use the regular Liquid Glass material in Standard and Immersive Mode.
  • Announcements can have several lines.
  • The finalized light tunnel and card corners in both modes.
